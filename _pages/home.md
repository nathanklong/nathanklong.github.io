### About me

Dr Nathan K Long is an Astrophotonics and Astronomical Instrumentation Researcher with the Astralis Instrumentation Consortium at the University of Sydney. He is leading the design of the Seidr instrument, a part of the Asgard Suite at the Very Large Telescope Interferometer, and working on the Photonic Wavefront Imager for the Magellan Clay MagAO-X. His multi-disciplinary work focuses on developing machine learning models to augment astronomical and optical instrumentation, quantum technology, and satellite communication. He obtained a 1st Class Honours in Aerospace Engineering at RMIT University (2018), a Master of Science in Computer Science at the University of New South Wales (UNSW) (2021), and a Doctorate on Machine Learning Assisted Continuous-Variable Quantum Communication at UNSW (2026). He is the Vice Chair of the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA) Sydney Section Committee and was previously the Deputy Chair of the Sydney Quantum Academy PhD Committee.
